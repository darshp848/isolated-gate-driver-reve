## GPIO header library

`hardware/libraries/GPIO_Headers.PcbLib` is the Altium conversion of KiCad standard `PinHeader_1x02_P2.54mm_Vertical` and `PinHeader_1x06_P2.54mm_Vertical` footprints and associated header models, with model-position corrections made during the Altium transfer. Attribution: KiCad library contributors; Altium conversion/placement corrections during Darsh Patel's project with Codex assistance.
